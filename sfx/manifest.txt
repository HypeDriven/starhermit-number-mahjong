# Number Mahjong — canonical SFX manifest
# file | event id | description | usage context
# Event ids are the keys of SAMPLE_FILES in js/app/audio.js; every id also has a
# procedural WebAudio fallback voice in AudioEngine.play().

ui-click.opus | ui | Short smooth ceramic button click — primary button press | js/app/main.js `_onScreenAction` default cue for menu buttons.
ui-hover.opus | hover | Very short soft ceramic tick — hover/focus on interactive elements | js/app/main.js pointerover on any screen-root button/input, throttled to 120 ms.
ui-confirm.opus | confirm | Calm two-note soft chime — confirm selection | js/app/main.js `_onScreenAction` for the "Start round" button.
ui-back.opus | back | Soft descending mellow tone — back navigation | js/app/main.js `_onScreenAction` for Back out of a non-settings screen.
ui-error.opus | invalid | Muted short double ceramic clack — pair doesn't satisfy the rule / denied | js/app/main.js on an `invalid` engine event and on rejected hint/shuffle/undo.
ui-success.opus | achievement | Gentle ascending three-note chime with warm shimmer — achievement unlocked | js/app/main.js `_showResults` when at least one achievement unlocks.
ui-modal-open.opus | modal-open | Soft airy whoosh with gentle chime — modal/panel opens | js/app/main.js pause-overlay Settings/Help buttons.
ui-toggle.opus | toggle | Small ceramic toggle click — settings toggle | js/app/main.js `_onSettingInput` for checkbox/select changes.
tile-select.opus | select | Single ivory tile lifted off wooden desk, soft ceramic click — tile selected | js/app/main.js `_afterCommand` on a `select` engine event.
pair-match.opus | pair | Two ivory tiles tapped together, bright ceramic clack with gentle chime — valid pair removed | js/app/main.js `_afterCommand` on a `pair` event with chain 1.
shuffle.opus | reshuffle | Many ceramic tiles shuffled on wooden table, soft clattering wash — board shuffle | js/app/main.js on a `reshuffle` event (R key or the Shuffle button).
board-clear.opus | board-clear | Calm warm gong swell with soft chime tail — board cleared / round complete | js/app/main.js `_onTerminal` when the round is won.
ui-tab-switch.opus | tab-switch | Small ceramic tab flipping over with a dry tick on wood — switching tabs in settings/results panels | js/app/main.js `_onScreenAction` when moving between top-level sections (Journey, Practice, Challenges, Learn, Score Chase).
ui-scroll-tick.opus | scroll-tick | Single soft notched tick of a wooden scroll wheel — scrolling lists (stage select, leaderboard) | js/app/main.js capture-phase scroll on screen-root, throttled to 150 ms.
ui-slider-drag.opus | slider | Ceramic knob sliding smoothly along a short brass rail — dragging volume/graphics sliders | js/app/main.js `_onSettingInput` for range (volume) changes.
ui-modal-close.opus | modal-close | Soft airy whoosh settling downward with a faint ceramic tap — modal/panel closes | js/app/main.js `_onScreenAction` for Back when returning to the pause overlay.
ui-toast-notify.opus | toast | Gentle rounded pop of a small ceramic bell tapped once — toast/notification appears | js/app/ui.js `toast()` for non-error toasts.
ui-pause.opus | pause | Heavy wooden drawer closing softly with a felt-dampened thud — game paused | js/app/main.js `pause()` (Esc, P, the pause button, or backgrounding).
ui-resume.opus | resume | Light bright ceramic tick with a tiny upward chirp — resume from pause | js/app/main.js `resume()`.
countdown-tick.opus | countdown | Single precise tick of a small brass observatory clock — pre-round countdown numbers (3-2-1) | js/app/main.js `beginRound` countdown, once per number 3-2-1.
timer-warning.opus | timer-warning | Small brass hand-bell struck once, urgent but restrained — final seconds of a timed challenge | js/app/main.js `_startTimerLoop`, once a second under 10.5 s remaining.
ui-settings-saved.opus | settings-saved | Rubber stamp pressed onto thick paper with a soft thump — settings saved / preferences applied | js/app/main.js `_onScreenAction` for Back out of the Settings screen.
tile-deselect.opus | deselect | Ivory tile set back down on the wooden desk with a soft low clack — tile deselected | js/app/main.js `_afterCommand` on a `deselect` engine event.
tile-drag-start.opus | drag-start | Ivory tile beginning to slide across polished wood, short smooth scrape — drag/pointer-capture starts on a tile | js/app/main.js canvas pointermove once the pointer passes the 14 px drag threshold.
tile-drop.opus | drop | Ivory tile placed into a shallow wooden recess with a snug soft knock — tile dropped onto a target slot | js/app/main.js canvas pointerup after a drag (no tile is chosen).
tile-expose.opus | expose | Ceramic tile sliding aside on wood revealing the tile beneath with a soft tick — deeper tile becomes available after a pair is removed | js/app/main.js `_afterCommand`, layered on top of `pair` when a removal frees a covered tile.
hint-glow.opus | hint | Soft shimmering glass-chime glimmer rising gently, like starlight in a lens — hint highlights a legal pair | js/app/main.js on a `hint` event (H key or the Hint button).
undo-move.opus | undo | Ceramic tile sliding backward across wood with a descending scrape and settling clack — undo last pair (practice mode) | js/app/main.js on an `undo` event (U key or the Undo button).
combo-chain-low.opus | chain (2-3) | Two quick bright ceramic clacks rising in pitch plus a small warm chime — short no-hint chain (2-3 pairs) | js/app/audio.js `play("chain")` when opts.chain < 4.
combo-chain-mid.opus | chain (4-5) | Rising run of three bright ceramic chime notes over a soft wooden knock — medium chain (4-5 pairs) | js/app/audio.js `play("chain")` when opts.chain is 4-5.
combo-chain-high.opus | chain (6+) | Jubilant ascending cascade of five ceramic/glass chime notes with shimmer tail — long chain (6+ pairs) | js/app/audio.js `play("chain")` when opts.chain >= 6.
round-start.opus | round-start | Single soft strike of a small brass singing bowl, calm swelling ring — round begins ("Observe") | js/app/main.js `beginRound` on the "Observe" beat before activation.
win-stinger.opus | win-stinger | Warm celebratory brass and ceramic chime fanfare rising to a sparkling held note — level/stage completed | js/app/main.js `_showResults` when the round is won.
lose-stinger.opus | lose | Gentle descending pair of muted wooden marimba notes fading out — level failed / moves or time exhausted | js/app/main.js `_onTerminal` on any losing terminal reason.
star-rating.opus | star-rating | Three small bright metallic dings in quick ascending order with glassy sparkle — star rating revealed on results screen | js/app/main.js `_showResults` when a journey stage awards stars.
new-record.opus | new-record | Sparkling ascending glissando of tiny glass chimes ending on a proud bright note — new personal best / leaderboard record | js/app/main.js `_showResults` when the run places first on a local board.
target-shift.opus | target-shift | Small brass instrument dial clicking one notch round with a soft glassy shimmer — the sum target changes after a pair in a dynamic-target round | js/app/main.js `_afterCommand` after a `pair` event in a dynamic-target round.
